Setup, inside Claude

No forms. You set it up by talking to Claude, once.

Type “Set up DAIGEST” and Claude walks you through six steps. Pick one to see how it goes.

    Get it

    Three steps and your first DAIGEST is on its way.

    1. 1

      Download the skill

      A small zip with DAIGEST's instructions for Claude. Download daigest.zip

      Source on GitHub.

    2. 2

      Add it to Claude

      Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, and upload the zip.

    3. 3

      Say the word

      Open Cowork and type Set up DAIGEST. Claude takes it from there.

    You need

    • A paid Claude plan with Cowork and scheduled tasks
    • Gmail, with Claude's Gmail connector turned on
    • Outlook and Hotmail are coming soon

    Your privacy

    • Your own Claude reads your newsletters. DAIGEST has no server that touches your inbox.
    • Only the senders you confirm as newsletters are read. Personal mail is left alone.
    • To stop, delete the scheduled task. Nothing else to cancel.
