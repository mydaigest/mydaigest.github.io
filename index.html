    Get it

    Three steps and your first DAIGEST is on its way.

    1. 1

      Download the skill

      A small zip with DAIGEST's instructions. Download daigest.zip

    2. 2

      Add it to Claude

      Upload it in Settings, Capabilities, Skills. Claude learns DAIGEST and waits until you ask.

    3. 3

      Start the setup

      In Cowork, type Set up DAIGEST. Claude asks six questions and sends your first mail.

    You need

    • A paid Claude plan with Cowork
    • Gmail, with Claude's Gmail connector on
    • Outlook and Hotmail: coming soon

    Your privacy

    • Your own Claude reads your mail, no DAIGEST server
    • Only the newsletters you confirm are read
    • To stop, delete the scheduled task
